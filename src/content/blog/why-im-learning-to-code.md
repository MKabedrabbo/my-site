---
title: "Why I am Learning to Code"
description: "I studied computer science in school, but I am now teaching myself to code with the goal of landing a job as a software developer."
pubDate: "Jul 11 2026"
---

I've always been into how computers work and what you can actually build with code. Learning to program is oddly satisfying too — it kind of feels like solving a puzzle, and eventually all the pieces just make sense.

I studied computer science in school but never finished. Life got in the way and I stepped back from it for a while. That interest never really left though, so I decided to just teach myself instead.

I put together a structured learning plan based on stuff that's actually used on the job, not just theory, and I've been working through it piece by piece — learning everything from how the internet actually works to building real projects.

The goal's pretty simple: keep learning, keep building, and eventually land a job as a software developer.
